About Azha Travel

Azha Travel is a trusted transportation company providing safe and reliable travel services for students across Egypt. We specialize in transporting students from Suez to various universities and destinations, ensuring comfortable and punctual journeys.

Our routes cover major universities including:

  • Galala University
  • Badr University
  • Russian University
  • And other universities in Egypt

We operate daily from 5pm to 9pm, providing convenient transportation options for students after their classes.
